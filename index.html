03 · Systems

The next month should begin with what the previous one already taught us.

I turned recurring decisions into visible systems so planning, training and follow-through did not have to restart from memory.

Planning

Connected occupancy, production, capture, ticket and revenue.

Monthly targets became weekly and daily decisions that could still change the result.

Enablement

Moved operational knowledge out of memory.

Checklists, wedding interview templates, camera standards and reporting structures made recurring work easier to execute and teach.

Diagnosis

“We are behind” became a question with an answer.

The operation could locate whether the gap came from volume, average ticket, capture, production or follow-through.

Training
