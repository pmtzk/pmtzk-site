04
Representation · Visual judgment

A place can be remarkable and still appear ordinary before arrival.

Working inside resorts made the distance between lived experience and online perception impossible to ignore.

↓
Room 04 · Perception before arrival

Drag the threshold between the place and its representation.

What exists

Quiet mornings. Staff who remember a name. A particular quality of light. The pace of the property. The feeling that remains after departure.

What can be perceived

A room type. A pool. A rating. Amenities. A price. Fragments that make distinct places easy to compare and difficult to remember.

That observation became EXIF Studio: research, writing and visual direction focused on the distance between what a place is and what a guest can perceive before booking.
